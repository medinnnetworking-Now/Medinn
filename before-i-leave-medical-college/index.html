Before I leave medical college.

Medical education from a student’s lens, written by a house surgeon at GMC Kozhikode, 290+ days into internship.

Red a critiqueBlack an insightBlue the evidence

The MBBS is the foundation of every doctor. Right now, it is undervalued.

This series starts there, with the gaps: in the learning experience, in clinical exposure, in research culture, and in the room we give students to explore, build and innovate.

The aim is to bring value back to undergraduate medical education, one article at a time, from the lens of someone still inside the system. First, strengthen what we already teach. Then, for students who want it, expand what it can enable. Clinical training stays the core.
