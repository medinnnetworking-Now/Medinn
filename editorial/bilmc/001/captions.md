## LinkedIn post (carousel as a PDF document)

What should a medical college really prepare us for?

The obvious answer is: to become doctors. But I keep asking whether that should be the limit of what it enables.

I'm 290+ days into my internship as a house surgeon at GMC Kozhikode. As I get closer to leaving, I'm starting a series on medical education from a student's lens: Before I Leave Medical College.

The first piece argues for two things together: improve the education we already provide, and expand what medical education can enable. Not another compulsory subject, but an environment where students who want to explore research, technology or entrepreneurship can do so without stepping outside medicine.

Full article: https://medinn.in/before-i-leave-medical-college/more-than-a-training-ground

If you're a student, intern, doctor or educator: what should a medical college enable beyond clinical training?
