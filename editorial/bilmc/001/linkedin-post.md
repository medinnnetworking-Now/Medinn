# LinkedIn post: Article 001 (attach the 12-slide PDF: export/bilmc-001-carousel.pdf)

Medical students are expected to have it all figured out.

On 25 October, at MedX Talks (the IMA MSN All Kerala event at GMC Kozhikode), I spoke about that pressure. It is also part of why I'm starting a new series.

I'm 290+ days into my internship as a house surgeon at GMC Kozhikode. Before I leave medical college, I want to ask, from a student's lens: what should a medical college really prepare us for?

Article 001: Medical College Is More Than a Training Ground for Doctors.

The short version:
- MBBS risks becoming a stepping stone to NEET-PG, rather than an experience we value in itself.
- Students see clinical problems every day, but have few places to take them.
- We don't need another compulsory subject. We need an environment where students who want to can explore research, technology and entrepreneurship.
- Improve the education we provide, and expand what it can enable.

Full article, with sources:
https://medinn.in/before-i-leave-medical-college/more-than-a-training-ground

If you're a student, intern, doctor or educator: what should a medical college enable beyond clinical training? I'd like to hear it.
