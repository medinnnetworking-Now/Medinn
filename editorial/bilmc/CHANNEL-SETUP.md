# Turn on the "Get each article on WhatsApp" button

The button is built and live in the code, but hidden until you give it your channel's invite link.

## 1. Create the channel (on your phone)
1. Open WhatsApp > **Updates** tab > **Channels** > **+** > **Create channel**.
2. Name: `Before I Leave Medical College`.
3. Description: `One article a week on reforming medical education, from a house surgeon's lens. By Dr. Sidharth Narayanan, GMC Kozhikode.`
4. Picture: `editorial/bilmc/channel-avatar.png` (white, black and red, made to fit WhatsApp's circle crop).
5. Tap **Create channel**.

Followers see the channel, not your phone number. Channels are one-way (people read, react, and can't message the whole group), which suits an article series.

## 2. Copy the invite link
Open the channel > its name at the top > **Share / Invite link** > copy. It looks like `https://whatsapp.com/channel/XXXXXXXXXXXX`.

## 3. Send me the link
Send me that link and I paste it into `essays/config.js` and publish. The button then appears in three places:
- under the title on the series page
- right after the photos at the top of each article
- in the black box at the end of each article (red button, first in line)

When the button is tapped, WhatsApp opens the channel and the person taps **Follow**. The page they came from stays open.

## 4. Posting each week
- Post the article link plus one line (the take) in the channel the same day you publish.
- Pin the channel link in your Instagram and LinkedIn profiles, and add "Get the next one: [channel link]" to every caption.
