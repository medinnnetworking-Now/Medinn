# Before I Leave Medical College: tone guide (applies to every article, post, carousel and poster)

## The premise
**MBBS is the foundation of every doctor's career, and right now it feels undervalued.**
This series exists to bring value back to undergraduate medical education: what we teach, how we learn, and, for those who want it, what it can lead to.

## Rules
1. **Lead with respect for clinical training.** Clinical practice is demanding and deeply valuable. Never imply it is lesser, narrow, "just" a stage or a consolation. The series never argues against becoming a good clinician.
2. **Red is for critique, and the critique aims at systems, gaps and attitudes**, such as "treated as a stepping stone", "no place to take a clinical problem", "no support to explore". Never colour red: "MBBS", "clinical practice", "training doctors", "learn medicine", "training ground", or any person.
3. **Every article makes both halves of the point, in this order:**
   (a) *Strengthen the core:* improve the education we already provide, and restore its value.
   (b) *Expand what it can enable:* for students who want it, room for research, technology and entrepreneurship, built on clinical strength, never as an escape from it.
4. **"Beyond the clinic" is additive and optional.** Say "a strong MBBS can enable…" and "for students who want to…". Never "more than just…".
5. **Words to prefer:** foundation, value, deserves, strengthen, build on, room to explore. **Words to avoid about the MBBS itself:** just, only, merely, stuck, waste, outdated, broken, a stepping stone (say "treated as a stepping stone" and aim the red at the attitude, not the degree).
6. **Reflective, questioning, constructive.** No invented statistics or quotes. Not more aggressive than the author's text.
7. **Colour code stays:** red = critique, black on white = insight, blue = evidence.

## Checklist before posting
- [ ] Does the first line say or imply the MBBS has value worth restoring?
- [ ] Is anything in red that is not a critique of a system, gap or attitude?
- [ ] Does any line read as "being a clinician is not enough"?
- [ ] Are both halves present: strengthen the core, then expand what it can enable?
- [ ] Are there any invented numbers or claims?
