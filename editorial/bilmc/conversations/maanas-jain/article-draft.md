## The problem (red, critique of a gap)
A standard laryngoscope costs about ₹1,000 to 2,000. In Dr. Jain's own video, a video-laryngoscope is shown at ₹2,00,000 to ₹3,00,000. A video view of the airway is useful for the person intubating, for the person learning, and for the record, yet it sits out of reach of many hospitals. [Author: check with him; do not call it an established market figure.]

## What he built (blue, evidence)
UVEAL, a universal video-enabling attachment for laryngoscopes, sends the view to a smartphone. On quintubate.com the company says it works with existing laryngoscopes, starts under ₹10,000, and is "CDSCO certified". Its video shows a real intubation at Civil Hospital, Gandhinagar.
