Most students start with the portfolio.

A lot of people get into undergraduate research to go abroad, write the USMLE, or clear PLAB or AMC.
That is a reason to start. It is not a reason to keep going.

MY START

Mine started before medical college.

I studied at PC Thomas Classes. Just before my drop year, an individual who believed in me quite a lot told me about whole-food, plant-based diets and veganism.
He talked to me about Dr. Michael Greger, Neal Barnard, and the Physicians Committee for Responsible Medicine.

MY START

He showed me Cowspiracy and Seaspiracy. A lot of it, in several instances, did not have solid scientific data behind it.
But whole-food, plant-based diets did have substance to them. I kept that close to my heart.
