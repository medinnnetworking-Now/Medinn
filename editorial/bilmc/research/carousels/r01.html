MY START

Mine started before medical college.

I studied at PC Thomas Classes. Just before my drop year, an individual who believed in me quite a lot told me about whole-food, plant-based diets and veganism.
He talked to me about Dr. Michael Greger, Neal Barnard, and the Physicians Committee for Responsible Medicine.
