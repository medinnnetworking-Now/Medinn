# CMC Analytica: Expression of Interest. Google Form writeup

## Form title
CMC Analytica: Expression of Interest (Research)

## Form description (paste under the title)
Wondering how to start in research?

CMC Analytica is open to UGs and PGs: everyone who is here to research, or trying to start out. How do you understand the basics, and work towards residency or future research? CMC Analytica is here to understand you: your questions, your queries and your preferences.

If you have topics or domains you want to work on, tell us, and we will build with you.

This takes about two minutes. We will use your details only to contact you about CMC Analytica.

CMC Analytica · Calicut Medical College · College Union · 2026–2027

## Questions (suggested)
1. Your name (short answer, required)
2. WhatsApp number or email (short answer, required)
3. College and batch (short answer)
4. Where are you now? (dropdown: MBBS 1st year, MBBS 2nd year, MBBS 3rd year, Final year, Intern, Post-intern, Other)
5. What are you looking for? (checkboxes: Learn the basics, Find a guide, Join a project, Prepare for PG or residency, Get ready for future research)
6. Research experience so far (multiple choice: None yet, A little, I have a project or paper)
7. Topics or domains you are interested in (paragraph)
8. Your question or query (paragraph)
9. How did you hear about us? (short answer)

## Confirmation message
Thank you. We have your interest and will be in touch.

## Settings
- Collect email addresses: off (question 2 covers it), or on if you prefer verified emails.
- Limit to 1 response: off (it forces a Google sign-in).
- Link responses to a Google Sheet.

## Launch announcement caption (post with the launch collage poster)
CMC Analytica is officially launched.

The CMC Alumni Association and the Yuga College Union 2026 of Calicut Medical College launched CMC Analytica to bring academic and higher research to the front of our college, and to help students understand the dynamics of research.

It is open to UGs and PGs: everyone who is here to research, or trying to start out. We hope to collaborate more and more, across years, departments and projects.

How it works:
1. Express your interest. We want to understand you and the domains you care about.
2. Get matched with prospective guides, postgraduates and teams, so you can work together on joint projects.
3. Attend workshops and evaluation sessions that deepen your understanding and build the framework and foundation for your research journey.

Express your interest: https://forms.gle/jCB1dBETjMWiza6K7 (link in bio, or scan the QR on the next poster)

Union representatives: Nayan Menon (Chairperson) and Benjamin Thomas (Vice Chairperson).
