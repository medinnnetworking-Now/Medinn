# CMC Analytica: Expression of Interest. Google Form writeup

## Form title
CMC Analytica: Expression of Interest (Research)

## Form description (paste under the title)
Wondering how to start in research?

How do you understand the basics, and work towards residency or future research? CMC Analytica is here to understand you: your questions, your queries and your preferences.

If you have topics or domains you want to work on, tell us, and we will build with you.

This takes about two minutes. We will use your details only to contact you about CMC Analytica.

CMC Analytica · Calicut Medical College · College Union · 2026–2027

## Questions (suggested)
1. Your name (short answer, required)
2. WhatsApp number or email (short answer, required)
3. College and batch (short answer)
4. Where are you now? (dropdown: MBBS 1st year, MBBS 2nd year, MBBS 3rd year, Final year, Intern, Post-intern, Other)
5. What are you looking for? (checkboxes: Learn the basics, Find a guide, Join a project, Prepare for PG or residency, Get ready for future research)
6. Research experience so far (multiple choice: None yet, A little, I have a project or paper)
7. Topics or domains you are interested in (paragraph)
8. Your question or query (paragraph)
9. How did you hear about us? (short answer)

## Confirmation message
Thank you. We have your interest and will be in touch.

## Settings
- Collect email addresses: off (question 2 covers it), or on if you prefer verified emails.
- Limit to 1 response: off (it forces a Google sign-in).
- Link responses to a Google Sheet.
