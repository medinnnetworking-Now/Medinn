## LinkedIn post (attach poh-launch-carousel.pdf)

I'm giving myself a challenge: connect with 50 MVPs of healthcare and MedTech in Kerala.

Healthcare is much bigger than hospitals. It's device manufacturers, diagnostics companies, hospital groups, biotech ventures, technology developers, researchers, investors, and clinicians building services. But how well do we actually know who is building what, right here in Kerala?

So I'm starting People of Healthcare, a Medinn initiative: 50 conversations with the founders, CEOs, key opinion leaders, hospital leaders, manufacturers and researchers shaping Kerala's healthcare ecosystem.

Through them I want to understand five things:
People: who is shaping healthcare in Kerala, and what have they built?
Production: what can Kerala develop and manufacture locally?
Talent: what skills exist, and what is missing?
Innovation: what does the road from a clinical problem to a product involve?
Pipelines: what funding, mentorship, partnerships and approvals does it take to execute?

There's one more reason. Doctors are often seen only as end users of healthcare products. They can also be collaborators, builders, researchers and advisors. To take part, they need to know the industry and the people in it. This is my way of helping make that accessible.
