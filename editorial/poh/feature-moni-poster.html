How do we deliver comprehensive cancer care to the people of Kerala?

Medical Director, Karkinos Healthcare

3 million patients
Building

Distributed cancer care: a platform from screening and diagnosis to treatment and follow-up, taking care out of the hospital and into the community.

The bottleneck

Large tertiary cancer centres were slow to adopt standardised care, and have little incentive to send patients back to community cancer clinics for follow-up closer to home.

Bridges to build

Digital health in primary clinics, and ABDM adoption in hospitals.

The future

Digital adoption will grow, and AI will help democratise quality cancer care.

The vision

A comprehensive cancer centre at the heart of a network of community cancer clinics, taking quality care to every community.
