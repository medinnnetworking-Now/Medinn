## LinkedIn caption (post only after he approves)
Building a healthcare startup is not like building any other tech startup.

I spoke with Hanan Kasim, a young founder in Kerala building Elara Health AI, an AI copilot for radiology reporting. His point stayed with me: the hardest part isn't the technology. It's getting access to the people and places where it has to prove itself. Hospitals willing to pilot it. Radiologists willing to tell him what's wrong with it. Institutional trust that takes time to build.

He says innovation alone isn't enough. Healthcare needs clinical trust, institutional access, regulatory awareness and real-world validation. And he believes Kerala has the clinical talent and technical potential to build globally competitive healthcare technology, if young founders get a real way in.

That's the gap we're trying to help close with People of Healthcare: building bridges between startups and hospitals in Kerala.
