# PoH: MVP / KOL feature questions (WhatsApp kit)

For the MVPs and KOLs who will be featured (with their photo). Fill-in page: https://medinn.in/people-of-healthcare-mvp
Mission line: Through People of Healthcare, Medinn is building bridges and widening adoption and collaboration between startups and hospitals in Kerala.
Rules as always: nothing published without their approval, no fee to be featured, Medinn is a consulting practice, no promises of funding or outcomes.

## Block A: introduction
Hello [Name], thank you for being part of People of Healthcare.

Through People of Healthcare, Medinn is building bridges and widening adoption and collaboration between startups and hospitals in Kerala: https://medinn.in/people-of-healthcare

I would like to feature what you are building and why it matters. You can fill in a short form here: https://medinn.in/people-of-healthcare-mvp
Or, if you prefer, just reply with voice notes and I will take it down myself.

Nothing is published without your approval, and there is no fee to be featured.

## Block B: about you (also ask for the photo)
First, so I can write it properly:
1. Your name, as you want it written
2. Your company or organisation
3. Your role
Please also send a photo of yourself that I can use in the feature.
(Optional: one number that shows your scale, whatever you are comfortable sharing.)

## Block C: if they prefer audio. Send one question at a time
1. What are you building or leading?
2. What is your biggest project, and what highlights do you want the world to know?
3. What are the bottlenecks you face?
4. What bridges need to be built? (between startups, hospitals, regulators, investors, colleges)
5. How can we increase adoption and discovery?
6. What is your immediate need right now?
7. Who do you need access to, to build better?
8. How do you see the future of healthcare?
9. What needs to be done?
10. Who should I speak to next? Would you introduce me?
11. Any comments for Medinn and People of Healthcare?

Audio line to send first:
"Voice notes are perfect. Take one question at a time, as long or short as you like. I will write it up and send you the draft for approval."

## Block D: thank you
Thank you, [Name]. This was very valuable. I will write up what you are building and what you see for Kerala's healthcare, and send you the draft with your photo before anything is published. If anything should stay off the record, tell me and it stays out.
