Why it matters for doctors

Doctors can be builders too.

Clinicians are often seen only as end users. They can also be collaborators, product developers, researchers and advisors.

To take part, they need to know the industry, the people in it, and how ideas become healthcare products.

People of Healthcare exists to make that knowledge accessible to clinicians and medical students.
