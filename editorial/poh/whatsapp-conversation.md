# PoH: WhatsApp conversation kit (one-day format)

For people who have replied to the poster or the discovery link. Sent in three short batches, not one wall of text. Aim: finish in one day, then decide fit and write the feature (sent to them for approval before publishing).

## 0. Facts check before sending (owner to confirm)
The intro below uses only facts that are in `internal/PROFILE.md`. These claims from the owner's draft are NOT yet backed there, so they are left out until confirmed:
- "First generation doctor": not in PROFILE.md. Add it there if true, then it can go in the intro.
- "5 lakhs in the last month" for the apparel company: PROFILE.md has Rs 2L+ direct sales (2025) and Rs 1.5L+ passive direct sales (2026) with KNYA Medical Apparel. Give the exact figure and period if it should be used.
- "Kenya": PROFILE.md names the company KNYA Medical Apparel (probably the dictation heard "Kenya"). Confirm.
- "50 lakh plus revenue": PROFILE.md says "Rs 50L+ revenue influenced for brands", led by Cytokine Lectures. The intro says "influenced", matching that.

## 1. Message 1: introduction (send alone; same text as card 1)
Hello, I would like to understand your work through a few questions.

I am Dr. Sidharth Narayanan, House Surgeon at GMC Kozhikode. I have spent 3+ years in medical edtech (Cytokine Lectures, where I became COO), have helped brands influence Rs 50 lakh+ in revenue across medical education, startups and medical apparel, and I am working on MedTech Malabar, bringing together healthcare workers, KOLs and MedTech founders in Kerala.

That is why Medinn started People of Healthcare: connecting with 50 people building things of value in healthcare and MedTech in Kerala.

I have a few questions. Reply here whenever it suits you, voice notes are welcome, and ask me anything back. I would like to finish this today.

If our aims line up, I will write a feature on what you are doing and why it matters for Malabar. You will see it before anything is published, there is no fee to be featured, and Medinn is a consulting practice (I say so openly). Nothing here is a promise of funding or outcomes.

## 2. Message 2: about you (prerequisite, send after they reply)
Before the questions, three quick things so I can describe your work properly:
1. Your name, as you want it written
2. Your venture or organisation, and your role
3. One number that shows its scale, whichever you are comfortable with: revenue, patients served, funding raised, team size, or years running. Nothing is published without your approval.

## 3. Message 3: the questions (light, answer in any order, voice notes welcome)
1. What are you building, and what problem made you start?
2. Where does your work get stuck in Kerala most: people, approvals, money, hospital adoption or supply?
3. What is your immediate need right now?
4. Who do you need access to, to build better?
5. Would access to a hospital ecosystem be useful to you?
6. What should we be building next in Kerala?
7. Who should I speak to next? Would you introduce me?

## 4. Message 4: thank you
Thank you, [Name]. This was exactly what I hoped to learn. I will write up what you are building and why it matters for Malabar, and send you the draft before anything is published. If anything should stay off the record, tell me and it stays out. And if you think of something I should have asked, just type it here.

## 5. Optional add-on by segment (pick 1 or 2 only, never all)
- Hospital founder / CEO: What does a hospital need to see before adopting a new product? Who pays in the end?
- Device / MedTech maker: Where do ideas stall: prototype, validation, approval, first customer, funding?
- Diagnostics / labs: What would make you pilot a Kerala-built solution?
- Researcher / KOL: Which clinical observation of yours could become a product, and what is missing?
- Investor: What do you rarely find in a Kerala healthcare founder?

## 6. Fit check (private, for Medinn only)
Tick: builds something real in Kerala healthcare or MedTech; open to a profile; gave a need, an access ask and one introduction; no conflict with what Medinn is. Log in the Notion nominations database (Status: Reviewing / Reached out / Done).

## 7. Handling replies
- Reply the same day, even with one line. Keep it light.
- Follow up on the one most specific thing they said.
- Never quote or publish without approval of the final text.
- Count only real, completed conversations in any public number.
