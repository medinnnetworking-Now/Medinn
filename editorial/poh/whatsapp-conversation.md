# PoH: WhatsApp conversation kit (one-day format)

For people who have replied to the poster or the discovery link. Sent in three short batches, not one wall of text. Aim: finish in one day, then decide fit and write the feature (sent to them for approval before publishing).

The intro below uses only facts that are in `internal/PROFILE.md`. These claims from the owner's draft are NOT yet backed there, so they are left out until confirmed:
- "First generation doctor": not in PROFILE.md. Add it there if true, then it can go in the intro.

## 1. Message 1: introduction (send alone)
Hello, I would like to understand your work through a few questions.

I am Dr. Sidharth Narayanan, House Surgeon at GMC Kozhikode. I have spent 3+ years in medical edtech (Cytokine Lectures, where I became COO), have helped brands influence Rs 50 lakh+ in revenue across medical education, startups and medical apparel, and I am working on MedTech Malabar, bringing together healthcare workers, KOLs and MedTech founders in Kerala.

That is why Medinn started People of Healthcare: connecting with 50 people building things of value in healthcare and MedTech in Kerala.

I have a few questions. Reply here whenever it suits you, voice notes are welcome, and ask me anything back. I would like to finish this today.

If our aims line up, I will write a feature on what you are doing and why it matters for Malabar. You will see it before anything is published, there is no fee to be featured, and Medinn is a consulting practice (I say so openly). Nothing here is a promise of funding or outcomes.

## 2. Message 2: the core questions (send after they reply to Message 1)
Please answer in whatever order you like.
1. What are you building or leading, in two lines, and what problem made you start?
2. What is the one thing you are proudest of so far, and the hardest stretch it took?
3. In Kerala today, where does your work get stuck: people, approvals, money, hospital adoption, or supply? Pick the biggest and tell me why.
4. What is one problem in Kerala's healthcare you wish someone would solve, that nobody is working on?
5. What do you still depend on other states or countries for, that Kerala could make or do itself?
6. Which role is hardest to hire (biomedical engineer, service technician, regulatory, quality, product, data), and what do fresh graduates or doctors lack when they join?
7. How do you work with clinicians today, and how could a doctor or house surgeon be useful to you (advisor, tester, researcher, co-builder)?
8. Who in Kerala should I speak to next, and would you be open to introducing me?

## 3. Message 3: close and fit check
9. What should I have asked you that I did not?
10. Is anything off the record, or something you would rather I do not publish?
11. Would you be open to a 20-minute call or a short session with medical students at GMC Kozhikode later? (Optional, say no freely.)

Then: "Thank you. I will send a draft of the feature for your approval before it goes anywhere."

## 4. Optional add-on by segment (pick 1 or 2 only, never all)
- Hospital founder / CEO: What does a hospital need to see before adopting a new product? Who pays in the end?
- Device / MedTech maker: Where do ideas stall: prototype, validation, approval, first customer, funding? Which approval step was hardest?
- Diagnostics / labs: What would make you pilot a Kerala-built solution, and what would you need from a clinician partner?
- Researcher / KOL: Which clinical observation of yours could become a product, and what is missing to take it forward?
- Investor: What do you look for in a Kerala healthcare founder that you rarely find? What stage is hardest to get funded?

## 5. Fit check (private, for Medinn only)
After the replies, tick: builds something real in Kerala healthcare or MedTech; open to a profile; gave at least one problem and one introduction; no conflict with what Medinn says it is. Log it in the Notion nominations database (Status: Reviewing / Reached out / Done).

## 6. Handling replies
- Reply the same day to every answer, even with one line.
- Ask follow-ups on the one most specific thing they said.
- Never quote or publish without their approval of the final text.
- Count only real, completed conversations in any public number.
