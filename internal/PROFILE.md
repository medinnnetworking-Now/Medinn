## 2. The Medinn story (told in your words, as published)

Medinn began as a way of meeting people. It was conceived to understand how innovation happens in medicine, and to build a network toward it, starting at the alma mater, Government Medical College, Kozhikode, and the institutes connected to it: NIT Calicut, IIM Kozhikode and other engineering and management institutes.

Then came a **makerspace next to the college** (TinkerSpace Calicut), where engineers were building around the clock, even in the dead of night. Then came the **IEDC at the medical college**, "as I imagined it, but better." Then the **Repair Café**, India's first medical one. That is now becoming **MedTech Malabar**.

Published as the first Medinn Newsletter issue and Medinn Blogs post: "The Medinn story, so far" (27 October 2026, 3 min read): https://medinn.in/blogs/the-medinn-story

## 8. Flagship projects

### The Medical Repair Café (18 October 2026)
- India's first medical Repair Café, at Government Medical College, Kozhikode, with IEDC GMC Kozhikode and TinkerSpace Calicut. 10 AM to 4 PM, 15 medical + 15 engineering seats, open to UGs and PGs.
- Medical students, CS and electrical engineers, TinkerHub makers and iHub biomedical engineers opened up condemned hospital devices on one bench.
- Results: a BPL patient monitor stuck on its boot screen reflowed back to live vitals; a Mindray SV300 ventilator taken apart and documented; a nebuliser, a BP cuff and NIV filters brought back. 5 devices repaired. Press: Malayala Manorama the next morning.
- Pull quote: "We look after our phones and computers better than the devices patients depend on."
- Page: https://medinn.in/repair-cafe ; 7-slide PDF report: https://medinn.in/repair-cafe-report.pdf
