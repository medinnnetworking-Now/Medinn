LESSON · EVIDENCE

Be skeptical, and read the evidence.

He showed me Cowspiracy and Seaspiracy. A lot of it, in several instances, did not have solid scientific data behind it.
But whole-food, plant-based diets did have substance. Separate the claim from the data. That is evidence-based thinking.

STEP 2 · LITERATURE REVIEW

Read before you plan. Do the literature review.

1
SearchPubMed and Google Scholar. Start with a few keywords.
2
ReadAbstracts first, then full texts of the relevant papers.
3
Find the gapWhat is already known, and what is not?
I started going into journals, looking up terms, learning what a review of literature was.
